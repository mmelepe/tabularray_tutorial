	\chapter*{Introduction}
	Creating tables in \LaTeX can be difficult. Luckily, in 2021 the tabularray package was created, offering a simple united interface for most functionalities you will ever need, and then some.
	
	\begin{infobox}{Target audience}
		This short version, non-official tutorial, is aimed at people who already have some experience in Latex and just want a quick reference to learn tabularray. It showcases examples of tabularray uses, with a small explanation.
		
		A more detailed tutorial for beginners (or people who wish to understand this package more in depth) is available at \url{https://github.com/mmelepe/tabularray_tutorial/blob/master/latest.pdf}.
	\end{infobox}
